## Run locally
Open `index.html` in a modern browser. No build step is required.

## Included frontend features
- Responsive layout and mobile navigation
- Product category filters and live search (Ctrl/Cmd + K)
- Sample product cards and a cart with quantity controls
- Contact details and a demo contact form
- Cart persistence in the browser where local storage is available

## Important demo limitations
- Product prices are placeholders/sample estimates. Confirm real prices, stock, exact laptop models, phone storage and accessory compatibility before publishing.
- The cart checkout does not submit an order or process payment. The contact form does not send email.
- A backend or commerce provider is required for real orders, payments, stock management and message delivery.
- The hero image uses an external image URL and requires an internet connection.


## Expanded catalogue and home-delivery order requests

The catalogue includes sample listings for smartphones, laptops and computer accessories, tablets, TVs and home electronics, audio products, gaming accessories, networking products, office equipment and cameras, and smart wearables. Search and category filters are available.

Customers can add products to the cart, enter their name, phone number and delivery address, and open a pre-filled WhatsApp order message to the store. The message must be reviewed and sent by the customer. This is an order-request flow, not a confirmed order or online payment system. It uses the store contact number configured in `script.js`; verify that the number is active on WhatsApp before publishing. Product prices are sample estimates, and delivery availability, fees, stock and final prices must be confirmed with the store. A real multi-user order database, inventory and payment processing would require a backend.
